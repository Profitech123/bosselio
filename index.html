What we do

A mortgage is a twenty-five year position. It deserves more than a comparison table.

Most UAE files are declined for reasons that have nothing to do with the rate: a bonus structure a bank will not annualise, a free-zone company in its first year, rental income counted at 80% by one lender and 50% by another, a name that triggers a compliance review.

We read the file first. Then we choose the two or three lenders whose credit policy fits it, and we tell you which one to take and why — including when the answer is to wait six months.

We are paid by the lender on completion. We will tell you the amount before you sign anything. If a cheaper route exists that pays us nothing, we will say so.

The process

Five chapters.

I

The reading

One hour, no fee

You send what you have. We read it and tell you what a credit officer will see — including the two or three things in it that will cause a question.

II

The shortlist

Two working days

Two or three lenders, chosen on credit policy rather than headline rate, with the ledger sheet and our recommendation in writing.

III

Pre-approval

Three to seven days

We assemble and submit the file. You sign once. Valid for sixty days, which is what gives you standing with a seller.

IV

Valuation and offer

One to two weeks

The bank values the property and issues the final offer letter. If the valuation lands short, we renegotiate or move lender — this is the chapter where advisors earn their keep.

V

Transfer

One day at the trustee

Registration at the Land Department, keys, and the mortgage noted on the title. We are in the room. Four to six weeks from the reading, on a file without surprises.
