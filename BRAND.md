# BOSSELIO — Creative Direction

**Category:** mortgage advisory, United Arab Emirates
**Positioning:** not a broker, not a comparison site — a *private mortgage office*
**Audience:** end-users and investors financing AED 2M–60M property in Dubai and Abu Dhabi; expatriate, self-employed, and non-resident buyers whose files do not fit a retail template
**One line:** *Private mortgage counsel.*

---

## 1. The strategic idea

### The problem with the category
UAE mortgage websites are built to harvest leads. They all share the same grammar: a stock skyline, a blue gradient, a smiling couple holding keys, a rate in a huge red circle, and a form that fires into a call centre. The visual language says *volume*. It says *we will call you six times*. For a client moving AED 8M, that language is actively disqualifying — it signals that they are a lead, not a client.

### The opposing idea
**Discretion is the luxury.** In private banking, the more valuable the relationship, the quieter the room. Our experience should feel less like a broker's storefront and more like the reception floor of a family office: limestone, low light, one person who already knows your file, and no one shouting a rate at you.

So the whole system is built on a single inversion:

> Everyone else sells *access to rates*. We offer *counsel on a decision*.

### Three principles that decide every detail
1. **Restraint over persuasion.** No badges, no countdown timers, no "trusted by 10,000+". One quiet proof point beats ten loud ones. If an element is trying to convince, delete it; if it is trying to *clarify*, keep it.
2. **Written, not shouted.** The site reads like a well-set private-client memorandum — editorial columns, real prose, numbered chapters. Typography does the persuading.
3. **Specific, not aspirational.** Never "your dream home". Always "a 62% LTV on a non-resident file, four weeks to offer". Precision is the flex. Vagueness is what the volume players sell.

### Voice
Measured, first-person-plural, structurally calm. Short declaratives. No exclamation marks, ever. No "!", no "unlock", no "journey", no "seamless", no "solutions". We say *file*, *counsel*, *terms*, *offer*, *structure*, *lender*. We name real constraints out loud — the DSR cap, the 20% down payment floor for expatriates, the cost of early settlement — because naming the constraint is what an advisor does and a salesman won't.

## 2. Visual identity

### The name in use
Set in Cormorant Garamond Light, **uppercase, tracked +0.38em**. The wide tracking is the signature: it forces the eye to walk the word slowly, which is the whole brand in one gesture. Never bold. Never italic. Never in a coloured box.

`B O S S E L I O`

Beneath it, at 1/6 the size, the descriptor in Inter, uppercase, tracked +0.22em: `PRIVATE MORTGAGE COUNSEL — UAE`.

### The mark: *The Aperture*
A single hairline square, rotated 45°, with its lower-right quadrant left open. It reads as (a) a door left ajar, (b) an aperture, (c) an unclosed file. Drawn at 1px at every size — it never fills, never gains a gradient, never sits inside a circle. In motion it draws itself in three strokes and stops one stroke short. The gap is the point: a mortgage is an open position until it closes.

### Bilingual lockup
Arabic sets **above** the Latin in Noto Kufi Arabic Light, never smaller than the Latin, never a translation afterthought. Aligned to the same optical left in LTR, mirrored wholesale in RTL. The hairline rule between the two scripts is 1px, 40% opacity — it joins, it does not divide.

## 3. Colour — *Desert Nocturne*

The palette is drawn from UAE architectural materials at the two hours when the light is worth looking at: blue hour and the ten minutes after sunset. No gold gradients. No royal blue. No "trust" teal.

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--obsidian` | `#0B0E0D` | Primary ground. Near-black with a green undertone, so it reads as *stone*, not as *tech dark mode*. |
| `--ink` | `#141917` | Raised surfaces, cards, the ledger. |
| `--oud` | `#1C2621` | Deep resinous green-black. Chapter grounds, hover states. |
| `--alabaster` | `#F4F1EA` | Primary type on dark; the paper ground in light passages. |
| `--sand` | `#D9D2C4` | Secondary type, hairlines at opacity, muted labels. |
| `--brass` | `#B08D57` | The single accent. Aged brass, not gold — it has grey in it. Used for **numerals, rules, and the mark**, almost never for large fills. |
| `--brass-lit` | `#D4B483` | Brass catching light. Hover and focus only. |
| `--terracotta` | `#9C5B42` | Reserved exclusively for cost, risk, and penalty figures. Appears perhaps twice per page. |

### The discipline
- Brass covers **under 4% of any viewport**. The moment it becomes a background fill, the brand becomes a Dubai real-estate flyer.
- Never place brass type on brass ground. Never place two accents adjacent.
- Hairlines are `--sand` at 12–20% opacity, exactly 1px, and they are the primary structural device — not boxes, not shadows. Luxury is lines, not borders.
- Light passages (the Ledger, the Counsel Card) invert to alabaster paper. The inversion *is* the emphasis; we never need a highlight colour.

## 4. Typography

| Role | Face | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Display | **Cormorant Garamond** 300 | `clamp(2.6rem, 7vw, 6.5rem)`, line-height 0.98, tracking −0.02em at large sizes. High stroke contrast does the work of ornament. |
| Editorial body | **Inter** 400 | 17–19px, line-height 1.75, measure capped at **66ch**. Never full-bleed text. |
| Labels / eyebrows | **Inter** 500 | 11–12px, uppercase, tracking +0.2em, `--sand` at 60%. |
| Numerals | **Inter** 400, `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` | Rates, LTVs, tenors. Tabular so figures align down a column like a ledger. |
| Arabic | **Noto Kufi Arabic** 300 | Paired at 1.06× the Latin size for optical parity. |

### Rules
- **Two faces. Ever.** A third face is a failure of nerve.
- Hierarchy comes from *scale and space*, never from weight. We own exactly one weight of the serif.
- Chapter numbers set as Roman numerals — `I`, `II`, `III` — in brass, at display scale, as compositional anchors rather than navigation.
- Rates are never larger than the sentence explaining them. This is the typographic expression of the whole positioning.

## 5. Imagery & art direction

### Never
Skylines with the Burj centred. Handshakes. Key handovers. Smiling families on sofas. Aerial Palm Jumeirah. Blue-tinted stock. Anything with a lens flare. Anything a competitor could also license.

### Always — *material detail at blue hour*
Photography is **cropped so tight that the subject becomes texture**: the shadow of a mashrabiya across travertine; brushed brass on a door pull; the joint where limestone meets water; a single palm frond against a raw concrete soffit; the underside of a cantilever. Architecture as evidence of care — which is what we are actually selling.

**Grade:** warm shadows, desaturated to roughly 35% chroma, lifted blacks (never crushed), a 4% monochrome grain overlay. Everything looks like it was shot on medium format at 6:40pm in November.

**Composition:** subject held at the edge of frame, two-thirds negative space, horizon or structural line aligned to the layout grid so the photograph and the type share one geometry.

**Interim:** until commissioned photography exists, this build uses no stock at all — compositions are drawn: layered SVG hairline geometry, a procedural grain, and slow light gradients. Drawn restraint outranks borrowed polish, and nothing here can appear on a competitor's site.

## 6. Composition & grid

- 12 columns, 96px outer margin on desktop, collapsing to 20px at 400px.
- **Asymmetry as default.** Content sits on columns 2–7 or 6–12, almost never 1–12 centred. The empty half is deliberate and must stay empty.
- A persistent 1px vertical hairline at column 2 runs the full document — a margin rule, as in a bound ledger. It is the spine of the whole experience.
- Vertical rhythm on an 8px base, with section breathing of 160–220px. Generous white space is the most expensive material available and it costs nothing.
- One idea per screen. If two things compete, the screen is wrong.

---

## 7. Atmosphere & motion

The feeling is **a quiet room at dusk**: low ambient light, a warm pool of it where you are working, no sudden movement.

- **Easing:** `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` — decelerating, never elastic. Nothing in this brand bounces.
- **Durations:** 700–1200ms for reveals, 240ms for interface feedback. Deliberately slower than convention; speed reads as urgency and urgency is the volume players' tone.
- Reveals are **rise + fade, 18px maximum**. No scale-in, no slide from off-screen, no parallax spectacle.
- Hairlines **draw** rather than appear — a rule scaling from its origin is the brand's core motion idiom.
- Numerals **settle** into place: counting up, decelerating, landing with a quiet 1-frame hold.
- A single ambient warm glow drifts almost imperceptibly behind the hero — 60s cycle, 3% opacity. It should be noticed only if looked for.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` returns the full composition instantly, at final state. Nothing is load-bearing on animation.

## 8. Signature moments

Five things a visitor remembers, none of which are on a competitor's site.

**I. The Aperture opening.** On first load, a crosshair of hairlines draws across the viewport, the mark completes its three strokes, and the wordmark's letter-spacing *settles inward* from +0.9em to +0.38em over 1100ms. It happens once per session, takes 1.6 seconds, and can be skipped with any input.

**II. The Ledger.** The lender comparison — the moment every competitor renders as a garish table of coloured logo cards. Ours inverts to alabaster paper: engraved rules, tabular numerals aligned down a true column, bank names set small in caps, and a brass rule that draws under the recommended line. Terms read as an *opinion*, with a one-line rationale per row in the advisor's own voice. It should feel like being handed a single sheet across a desk.

**III. The Position.** A live figure the client can move — property value, tenor, residency status — that recomputes indicative monthly cost and returns *one* number, large, with the constraint that binds it named underneath in plain language ("your DSR, not the LTV, is what limits this file"). A calculator that argues back, rather than one that flatters.

**IV. Chapters.** The advisory process as numbered chapters `I`–`V`, each a single screen, each with one sentence of substance and one honest duration. The brass Roman numeral is the compositional anchor. No progress bars, no checkmark icons.

**V. The Counsel Card.** In place of a lead form: a card, inverted to paper, with three fields and a sentence naming what actually happens next and who reads it. Submission draws a hairline seal rather than showing a spinner. Copy: *"One advisor reads this. You will hear from them, once, within a working day."*

## 9. Interaction craft

- Focus states are **brass hairline outlines at 2px offset**, fully visible — accessibility rendered as a design asset, not a default ring suppressed by shame.
- Links underline on a 1px brass rule that draws from the left on hover, 240ms.
- Cursor: default. Custom cursors are a tell.
- No modals. No cookie wall theatre. No chat bubble. No exit-intent anything. Each of these is a small betrayal of the premise.
- Hit targets 44px minimum; tap feedback is a hairline, not a colour flood.

## 10. Anti-brief — instant fails

If any of the following appears, the work has reverted to template:
gold gradient · glassmorphism card · drop shadow used for hierarchy · emoji · gradient text · badge row · testimonial carousel · "Get Started" · "Trusted by" · sticky CTA bar · animated counter with a plus sign · fully centred hero with a photo behind it · more than one accent colour on screen · bold serif · two typefaces plus a third for "accents" · exclamation marks · the word *seamless*.
